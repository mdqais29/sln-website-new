About SLN Transportation

Dedicated to Safe Student Journeys Every Single Day

SLN Transportation is a trusted school transportation provider based in Des Plaines, Illinois. We specialize in providing safe, reliable, and comfortable door-to-door transportation for students across Cook County.

Our core priority is simple: deliver complete peace of mind to parents, schools, and school districts through background-checked drivers, attentive monitors, and routinely inspected climate-controlled vehicles.
